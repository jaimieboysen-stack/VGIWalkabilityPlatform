About

This platform was developed to collect small-scale data on street walkability using volunteered geographic information (VGI). Anyone can contribute by navigating to the contribution page and sharing their observations.

Terms of use

This is a research prototype for street walkability data. Users are responsible for uploaded content and must have the rights to share it. Data is for research use only. Scraping or reuse is not allowed.
